Per https://pocketbase.io/docs/api-rules-and-filters/ the eight "?" operators share one gloss:
  "?!= Any/At least one of NOT equal"   /   "?!~ Any/At least one of NOT Like/Contains"  (etc.)

OBSERVED: of the eight, ?!= alone reports a match for a record whose relation is empty.
Its match-all counterpart != does the same, while !~ / ?!~ do not.
A record with zero related items has no item that "is not news", so ?= and ?!= both being
false for C would be self-consistent; ?= false + ?!= true is not.
